# A friend gave you a book

友達が本を＿＿。Which giving verb fills the blank, and why not the other one?

<!-- hint -->
The two verbs differ in the direction the book moves relative to the speaker.

<!-- back -->
くれた. くれる is used when the receiver is the speaker or someone in the speaker's own group; あげる describes giving away from that group, so it cannot take the speaker as its receiver.

<!-- note -->
Makino and Tsutsui, A Dictionary of Basic Japanese Grammar, entries あげる and くれる.
