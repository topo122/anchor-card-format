# Te-form of a godan verb ending in く, ぐ or す

What replaces the final syllable in each case?

<!-- hint -->
Only one of the three is voiced, and it is voiced because the syllable it replaces was.

<!-- back -->
いて, いで and して respectively. 書く → 書いて, 泳ぐ → 泳いで, 話す → 話して.

行く is the exception: 行って, not 行いて.

![[01M1AK97PVJA1Q063702FFQ5JN]]

<!-- note -->
す keeps a full syllable rather than reducing to い, because the te-form here attaches to the regular 連用形 話し.
