# Te-form of a godan verb ending in う, つ or る

What replaces the final syllable?

<!-- back -->
って. 買う → 買って, 待つ → 待って, 取る → 取って.

![[01M1AK97PVJA1Q063702FFQ5JN]]

<!-- note -->
帰る and 入る end in る but are godan, so they follow this card and not the ichidan rule: 帰って, 入って.
