# Counting people up to three

How are 一人, 二人 and 三人 read?

<!-- back -->
ひとり, ふたり, さんにん. Only the first two are irregular; from three on the counter is the regular ～にん.

<!-- note -->
四人 is よにん, not よんにん — the other reading in this series that has to be learned separately.
