# How the te-form is built

The te-form is derived from the dictionary form, and which rule applies depends on the class of verb.

Ichidan verbs (る-verbs) drop る and add て: 食べる → 食べて, 見る → 見て. する and 来る are irregular: して and きて.

Godan verbs (う-verbs) instead change their final syllable, and the change depends on which syllable that is. Those are the rules worth drilling one at a time.

Each of them doubles as a rule for the plain past た: wherever the te-form takes て the past takes た, and wherever it takes で the past takes だ.
