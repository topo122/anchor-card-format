# The negative of 知っています

If 知っています is "I know", how do you say "I do not know"?

<!-- back -->
知りません, plain form 知らない. The negated ている form 知っていません is not used for this meaning.

<!-- note -->
知る is a change-of-state verb, so ている names the state of having come to know something; the plain negative covers the absence of that state.
