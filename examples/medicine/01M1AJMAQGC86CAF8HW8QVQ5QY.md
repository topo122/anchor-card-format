# Orthopnea

<!-- back -->
Breathlessness that appears when the patient lies flat and is relieved by sitting or standing up.

<!-- note -->
Lying flat shifts blood from the legs and the splanchnic circulation into the thorax; when the left ventricle cannot accommodate the extra venous return, pulmonary venous pressure rises. Patients usually describe the symptom as the number of pillows they need in order to sleep.
