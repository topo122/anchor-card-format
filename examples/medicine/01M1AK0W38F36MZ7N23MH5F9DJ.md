# Why conduction is slow through the atrioventricular node

What do the two consequences of the delay achieve?

<!-- hint -->
One concerns the order of events in a single beat; the other concerns what happens when the atrial rate is very high.

<!-- back -->
Conduction through the node is the slowest in the heart, giving a pause of roughly a tenth of a second between atrial and ventricular depolarisation.

1. **Filling.** Atrial contraction finishes before the ventricles contract, so the ventricles are fully loaded when they eject.
2. **Protection.** The node has a long refractory period, so it fails to conduct some impulses when the atrial rate is very high, and the ventricular rate stays lower than the atrial rate.

<!-- note -->
On the surface electrocardiogram the delay is what the PR interval measures.
