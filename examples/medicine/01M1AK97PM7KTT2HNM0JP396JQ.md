# Phase 4 of the ventricular action potential

What holds the cell at its resting potential between beats?

<!-- back -->
The inward rectifier **potassium** current (IK1), which conducts well at negative potentials and holds the membrane close to the potassium equilibrium potential, about -90 mV. The gradients it works on are maintained by the Na+/K+-ATPase.

A ventricular myocyte has no pacemaker current, so its phase 4 is flat and the cell waits to be depolarised by its neighbours.
