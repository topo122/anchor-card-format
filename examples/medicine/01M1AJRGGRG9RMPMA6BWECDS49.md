# Virchow's triad

Name the three categories, with one example of each.

<!-- back -->
1. **Endothelial injury** — trauma, surgery, indwelling vascular catheters
2. **Stasis or turbulent flow** — prolonged immobility, atrial fibrillation
3. **Hypercoagulability** — malignancy, pregnancy, inherited thrombophilia

Together they describe the conditions that predispose to thrombus formation. Most clinical situations combine more than one.

<!-- note -->
Named after Rudolf Virchow; the three-part grouping is a later restatement of his work rather than his own wording.
