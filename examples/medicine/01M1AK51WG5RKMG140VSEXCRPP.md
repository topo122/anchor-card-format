# Upper versus lower motor neuron lesions

How do the two differ on examination?

<!-- back -->
| Finding | Upper motor neuron | Lower motor neuron |
|---|---|---|
| Tone | Increased, spastic | Decreased, flaccid |
| Tendon reflexes | Brisk | Reduced or absent |
| Plantar response | Extensor | Flexor |
| Wasting | Slight, from disuse | Marked |
| Fasciculations | Absent | Present |

Weakness occurs in both, so it does not separate them; the accompanying signs do. Immediately after an acute upper motor neuron lesion the limb may be flaccid and areflexic, and the spastic picture appears over the following days or weeks.
